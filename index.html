Good to Know

A few notes for the resort leg.

🌊 Make the Resort the Trip

Days 4 and 7 are deliberately open — pool, beach, sunset, repeat. The 25% F&B discount sits on your first booking (27–30), so it covers Missoni and Bejana but not the Beach Grill on the 31st (different package). Food only, never alcohol — so dine freely and price the wine separately.

🚗 Two Outings, Two Transport Plans

Day 5 (El Kabron): Grab out, El Kabron arranges the ride back — no fixed schedule. Day 6 (Uluwatu): a full-day private driver (Innova-class, via RC) since it's multi-stop. Grab is patchy in the Uluwatu cliffs at night, so a waiting car earns its keep there.

✦ Marriott Titanium

They've already flagged your status and your room preferences are with the team. This property is known to be generous with upgrades for Titanium — but they're never guaranteed, so treat one as a happy bonus. Expect a welcome amenity and late checkout (request up to 16:00 on the 1st). Note: the Club Lounge here is a paid add-on, not a Bonvoy benefit.

🛕 Two Temples

Batuan (Day 3) and Uluwatu (Day 6). Sarongs are provided at both gates — covered knees and shoulders required. At Uluwatu, guard sunglasses and phones from the monkeys.

💳 Cash & Cards

IDR cash is handy for temples, tips, Grab, and the day trips. ATMs at DPS or near the resort. RC and major spots take Visa/Mastercard.

🌤️ Late-July Weather

Dry season — sunny, low humidity, ~27–30°C, almost no rain. Perfect beach and sunset weather. Pack light, breathable, reef-safe sunscreen.

Slow days, good wine, better sunsets.
