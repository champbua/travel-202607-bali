💡 Good to Know
🛵
Scooter Rental (Day 4)

Arrange with Renaissance concierge the evening of Day 3. Bring your international license. Helmet required. Wear sunscreen — it's exposed riding around Uluwatu.

🧣
Temple Dress Code

Bring a sarong for temple visits (Batuan, Uluwatu). Most temples loan or sell them at the entrance. Covered knees and shoulders are required.

💳
Cash & Cards

IDR cash is handy for waru ngs, temples, tips, and scooter fuel. ATMs at DPS airport or near hotels. Major restaurants accept Visa/Mastercard.

🚗
Grab App

Download Grab (Southeast Asia's Uber) before arrival. Good for casual transfers where no private car is arranged. Make sure your Singaporean/Thai SIM or local SIM works for data.

✦
Marriott Titanium Perks

Mention Titanium at both hotel check-ins. Expect: room upgrades, lounge access, welcome amenity, late checkout (request up to 16:00). At Double Ikat — mention Titanium for table acknowledgement.

🌤️
July Weather

July is Bali's dry season — sunny, low humidity, warm (~27–30°C). Almost zero rain. Perfect beach and outdoor weather. Pack light breathable clothing and reef-safe sunscreen.
